October in the Pro Shop

Pro Shop hours through October 7 will
remain 9 am-12 noon. We will resume
our normal hours of 7am to 5pm
beginning October 8. The re-grip
special will be continued through the
month of October. The normal $3 per
grip labor charge will be reduced to $2
per grip. We have a wide variety of
grips for you to choose from and will
be happy to assist you finding the right
grip. Typically, there is a two-day lead
time for receiving grips from the
manufacturer and then 1 day for the
actual re-gripping. Get those worn
grips re-gripped and watch those
scores improve.

October means close- outs in the pro
shop. Look for discounts on apparel,
shoes and accessories during this
month.

October Events

Our first big event in October is the
RUN/WALK THE VIEWS 5K on
Saturday October 6 @ 8am. Same
day registration and gift packet pick up
begins @6:30am. All proceeds will be
going to The First Tee of Tucson.
Long Sleeve Antigua t- shirts with the
race logo on the sleeve are available
for $10. The race will be run on the

front nine of our course and the start
finish line will be located in the cart
parking area in front of the restaurant.
If you are not running or walking,
come on down for some breakfast and
watch and enjoy all the festivities.

On October 28 from 4-5:30pm we will
be hosting The Views Halloween Cart
& Treat. Residents are asked to
decorate their carts and hand out
candy to children in the golf cart
staging area. There will be a contest
for the best decorated cart. For
participating, you will receive 1
complimentary guest pass for 18 holes
of golf. We will wrap up the evening
with a Glow Ball chipping and putting
contest at 6pm. Don’t miss out on this
fun time.

Is your cart in need of a good
cleaning? If you were at Cart & Treat
it might need one... On Monday
October 29 from 1 - 5pm_ our
professional outside services staff will
be offering FREE cart detailing. Clean
carts are happy carts! Who will be
first in line?

Pro Tip

Many of us have taken the last few
weeks off from golf. Remember to do
some light stretching as part of your
warm up. Those first swings after a
layoff can catch us off guard so be
aware or beware! As for our golf
swings; those first few times out, just
try to make solid contact and swing
through the ball, nothing more. You'll
be surprised that if you keep things
simple you will be shaking off that rust
sooner than you expected. Have fun
and hit em straight!
