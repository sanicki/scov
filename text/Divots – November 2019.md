The Gate System

As we come off overseed and cart path only
restrictions, we have implemented the Gate
System. Golfers must now enter through the
gates (blue stakes) nearest the tee box and
keep carts in the fairway until exiting through
the gates nearest the green. Please be
respectful of this to ensure the excellent
conditions we have grown accustomed to.
Sand and seed mixture is now available to
use for filling your divots in the fairway only.
The second hole has not been overseeded.
Please refrain from filling divots on hole #2.
Thank you!

A Pet Peeve

Do you know the proper way to fill a divot?
When filling a divot with your sand and seed
mixture, please do your best to fill the divot
even with the surrounding turf. This will allow
the seed to take easier and protect the mower
blades. As play increases, let’s all do our best
to fill those divots. Thank you!
