Pro Tip
From Tim Lochner

The downtown effect

One of the keys to putting is
understanding the “downtown
effect”. The Views Golf Club was
built in such a way that it has a slight
tilt toward downtown Tucson. This
will affect the speed of your putts
when putting towards Tucson. They
will be faster. The opposite will be
true putting away from Tucson. It
will also effect the break when
putting west to east (left to right) or
east to west (right to left). When in
doubt, remember the “downtown
effect”.
