Holidays

The Views Golf Club staff wishes all our
residents and guests a very merry holiday
season and a happy and healthy New Year.
We are grateful for your support throughout
2019 and look forward to an exciting and
challenging 2020.

December brings with it the beginning of
colder temperatures and the possibility of
frost delays. As a reminder, our “frost line” is
520-825-0322. This line will provide you with
any weather-related delays to your tee
times. For live updates, go to our website at
TheViewsGolfClub.com. Your initially
delayed tee time may be adjusted to an
earlier time as we have cancellations that
will affect your time.

As a reminder, we are using the gate system
for entering and exiting the fairways. Please
drive your cart on the fairway and drive in
the rough only when entering and exiting.
We will remain cart path only on all par 3’s.
Thank you for your cooperation and support.
Let's also remember to fix our divots in the
fairways and to fix our ball marks and one
other on every green.

From the Mats

In an effort to improve the condition of the
driving range and to allow new growth, the
driving range will be mats only from
December 9 - 31. Your cooperation during
this time is greatly appreciated. Thank you.

Results

The Views Golf Club hosted the 2019 Pro
Member Invitational on November 25.
Fourteen area pros competed in four-person
teams in a one ball gross one ball net per
hole format. The pros also competed in a
stroke play event and skins contest.

Winners in the team event with a score of 18
under par were Pro Jay Synkelma and our
very own Eileen Buskirk, Rob Ranson, and
Duane Alstrom. The team of Pro Tim
Lochner and amateurs Gary McCarrell, Bob
Ranson, and John Olson carded a 17 under
par to claim 2™ place. Finishing 3” at 16
under par was the team of Pro Tom
Mackinney amateurs Doug Kimble, Jim
Mitchell, and Matthew Mackinney. Special
thanks go out to Charley Young for a great
job on the grill.

Chris Dompier shot a two-under 70 to claim
the top spot in the Pro Division.
Representing The Views; Tom MacKinney
shot a three-over 75, Director of Golf Rick
Price shot 76 and Tim Lochner shot 78.

Our Annual Couples Tournament was held
on Sunday, December 1. In the 18-hole
division, the winners were Audrey Salkield
and Robert Taylor, 2™ place went to Eileen
Buskirk and John Olson with 3° going to the
team of Bob and Rob Ranson. The nine-
hole division was captured by the team of
Barb Vega and Cliff Miller. Great job to all
who participated.

Shots Heard Around The Views

Pat Kolley scored an ace on the 7" hole. He
used an eight-iron from 140 yds on this
challenging par 3. The shot was witnessed
by Don Bong and Bill Smith.
Congratulations, Pat!
