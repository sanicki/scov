Summer is Coming

The warm weather is finally here. With the
warmer temperatures comes allergy season
and the first appearances of our favorite
reptile, the rattlesnake. We must all be
careful and give them a wide berth. If you
must go into the desert, and we recommend
you don't, look first for the snakes and second
for your ball. A golf ball is not worth a snake
bite. As a reminder the rules of golf now
recommend a local rule that if your ball goes
into the desert you may place the ball in the
fairway nearest to where it went out of play
with a 2-stroke penalty. This will eliminate the
need for entering the desert areas and keep
you safe from the unseen critters lurking in
wait. Be safe and have fun!

We will miss all our winter friends as they
head back North. Thank you for helping
make The Views Golf Club the place to play
in Southern Arizona.

To those of you staying with us through the
summer months, we look forward to
continuing to make your golf experience the
best that we can. Let’s have a great summer!

A Tip from the Pro

Did you know that the average PGA Tour
player practices the short game 80% of their
total practice time? What's your percentage?
If you're like most of us, it's probably the
opposite, or 20% of your practice time. Try
switching that around and see what happens.
If you're still having trouble, come join us for
our Wednesday morning 8 AM clinics either
for full swing or short game.
