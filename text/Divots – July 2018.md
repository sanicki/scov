From the Golf Shop
Tom MacKinney, PGA Associate

July is upon us and if you are wondering
where the monsoons are, you are not
alone. Rest assured the monsoons will
arrive and when they do here are some
things to remember. When you see
lightning it is time to get off the course.
Lightning does not have to be near you to
injure or worse. Head home, to the pro
shop or restaurant until the storm passes.
Do not enter washes when flooded. A
depth of only 6 inches of fast moving water
can sweep a car away. Think what that
would do to a golf cart. So, let’s be aware
of our surroundings and always error on
the side of caution.

From the Range to the Course

A very common lament we hear is the
inability for players to take their range
session to the golf course. Here is a fun
and simple tip to help you have a more
seaniless transition from the range to the
course. On the range there is no fear
factor. A bad shot doesn’t ruin a hole. We
simply put another ball down and hit again.
This allows for a freer swing with less
tension allowing the club head to release
properly. There is no trouble left, right
and short that there is on that first tee.
Instead of worrying about taking our game
from the range to the course let’s bring the
course to the range.

When you are on the range play the
course in your mind. Visualize that first
tee shot and picture it on the range. Hit
the club you normally would on the first

tee and give it a relaxed confident swing. If

you hit your imaginary fairway or close to it
imagine the next shot and hit the
appropriate club. Play 9 holes or the
whole course like this. While on the range
make sure to go through a quick pre-shot
routine on these shots. Mirror what you do
on the course. This will help you take your
game from the range to the course. Try it!
