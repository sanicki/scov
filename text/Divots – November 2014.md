Bunker Etiquette

The question of where to put the
rake (in the bunker or out) has
always been an issue. The PGA
recommends that you leave the
handle of the rake outside the
bunker and the rake part inside
the bunker. This will make it
easier for the next person to use

As most of you already know we
have recently changed the range
ball dispensing machine. Some
that have gotten the cards have
had some difficulties using them.
So, I wanted to go over the new
procedures for you. On_ the
machine front you will find 4
silver buttons right above the
number key pad. The first order
of business is to press one of the
silver buttons. It doesn’t matter
which one as they are all
programmed the same. The
screen on the machine will show
a selection number depending
on which button is depressed. It
doesn’t matter. Then the screen
will switch to showing “small $5”.
When you see that on the screen
it’s time to insert the card in the
reader slot. This is the critical
point where the problems are
happening. There is a number
sequence on the back of the
card. That number sequence is
in the magnetic strip. If the card
is not fully inserted until the card

hits the back of the reader then
the reader only picks up part of
the number sequence. This
makes a_ difference as the
machine has a transmitter which
transmits the number sequence
to our computer in the golf shop.
The number transmitted is
compared to the numbers stored
in the software in the computer.
Tf it can’t find a match then the
display “link

means the

machine will
timeout” This
software could not find a number
match to verify the validity of the
card being used. Bottom line is,
make sure you insert your card
all the way into the reader until it
stops at the back. Then pull it
out of the reader. This does not
have to be done really fast. I
hope this helps.
