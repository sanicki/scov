Tip from the Pro

It's that time of year when, because of
overseeding, we are forced to hit off
mats. When we hit off mats, we can
get a false read on our ball striking.
The club can skip off the mat into the
ball, still giving a good ball flight and
adequate distance. When we are
back on regular turf, this same impact
point will cause a fat or heavy shot
lacking in distance. Try to concentrate
on the ball first and then mat contact.
Execute this, and you'll feel and hear a
well-struck ball with no false reading.
Try this!
