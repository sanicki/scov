SCOV Solar Project Celebrates
Its 10-Year Anniversary

By Dale Foster

SCOV's solar project is about to turn ten years old and
it's a moment worth celebrating. What began in 2016 has
grown into one of the most successful improvements to our
community.

Beginning on November 1, 2016, the original solar instal-
lation totaled 426 kilowatts (kW) across eight facilities. Solar
panels were placed on top steel parking structures in the
Mountain View complex with the rest mounted onto roof-
tops. Development and installation was at no cost to SCOV
through a partnership with a Tucson based solar company.
SCOV purchased this solar energy at a rate slightly below
TEP's prices and the cost savings totaled about $75,000
over the first five years.

During November of 2021, the SCOV Board made a ma-
jor step by purchasing the solar systems for $664,000. This
boosted annual savings to $145,000. In 2025, a ninth solar
system was added at the AFC increasing our solar capacity
to 532 kW. A typical SCOV home system is 5 kW. The pro-
gram has now saved our community about $775,000 with
current annual savings near $175,000.

These solar panels have been exceptionally reliable. Of
the 28 electronic inverters, three have needed replacement
and were covered under warranty. They consistently pro-
duce as much clean energy today as a decade ago.

After 10 successful years, this project has been widely ap-
preciated by residents and visitors who value costs, shaded
parking, indoor facility comfort and the progressive spirit that
sets our active retirement community apart from the others.

Common Space Initiative:
Building for SCOV’s Future

Over the past month, you've read about the
Common Space Initiative, a comprehensive
plan to address SCOV's growing space needs
while updating three of our major buildings. This is the first
time in our 40-year history that we've taken this communi-
ty-wide approach. This initiative will:

*Add space for groups that need it across three buildings:
Fused/Stained Glass, Silver & Lapidary, the sewing clubs,
Wood Shop, Clay Club and Library, Loan Room and Cos-
tume Closet.

+Provide space for groups that have been waiting, includ-
ing Carvers and Table Tennis. Return space to the Golf
Program.

*Update three major SCOV buildings to better serve our
community.

The first project is the Copper Center, purchased in 2023
and now fully paid for. It will house clubs and services mov-
ing from the Artisan Center, Welcome Center, and Cart Barn.
This, in turn, sets the stage for remodeling the Artisan Cen-
ter to accommodate growing Clay Club and Wood Shop par-
ticipation, to expand the Gift Shop and provide dedicated
space for Carvers. The Welcome Center can then expand
the Library, the community's second most-used amenity,
and provide additional administrative space. And freeing up
space in the Cart Barn for the golf operation.

Updating our amenities and buildings is an investment in
SCOV's future. Our location and amenities are our market
niche. Let's build on that strength.

Lighting & Landscaping Update

The Town of Oro Valley (the Town) deter-
mined that SCOV has actually owned the light
poles along the boulevard all along. The As-
sociation will therefore be responsible for determining the
next steps for the existing lighting. During this process, the
Board has approved transferring responsibility for landscap-
ing along Rancho Vistoso Boulevard to the Town.

As part of the landscaping transition, the Board is looking
for opportunities to reduce water usage and costs and will
evaluate turning off five irrigation meters. While reducing irri-
gation, attention will be given to maintaining the appearance
of SCOV's entry monuments and other key focal areas, with
an emphasis on attractive, low-water-use landscaping.

The existing light poles are expected to be removed once
the Town provides the Association with a Memorandum of
Understanding outlining the light pole ownership and the
transition of landscaping responsibilities. The Town is tenta-
tively planning to add lighting at the crosswalks along Ran-
cho Vistoso Boulevard, which will provide targeted lighting
where it is most needed. You will be kept updated as this
project progresses.

Exercise Keeps Your Metabolism High

Metabolism naturally slows as you age, so it’s harder to
avoid pounds creeping up as the years pass. Luckily, sched-
uling regular workout sessions helps you increase the total
number of calories you burn, helping you maintain a steady,
healthy weight. You'll torch more calories if you add in resis-
tance training to your routine at least a few times a week,
since working out with free weights or doing bodyweight
exercises helps build muscle mass. The more muscle you
have, the higher your calorie burn is and it stays higher all
day long, even after you've stopped exercising.

Architectural Inspection Group

Lisa Sandrock 520-917-8072 lsandrock@suncityorovalley.com

When a home in Sun City Oro Valley is listed for sale or
goes into escrow, the Architectural Inspection Group (AIG)
plays an important role in the process. AIG conducts an ex-
terior inspection of the property to help ensure that the home
and surrounding property comply with the community's cur-
rent architectural standards and guidelines. The inspection
helps identify items that may need attention before the sale
is completed. The inspection includes the home's exterior,
landscaping, fencing, paint, walls and other visible improve-
ments that are subject to community standards. If an issue
is identified, the homeowner may be notified so it can be
addressed. Homeowners can help make the selling process
smoother by keeping their property well maintained and
making sure that any exterior improvements have received
the appropriate approval from the Architectural Review
Committee (ARC), when required. AIG inspections are an
important part of maintaining the appearance and standards
of Sun City Oro Valley while helping provide buyers and sell-
ers with a clear understanding of the property's compliance
during the sale process. If you are considering selling your
home, it is a good idea to review your property and address
any potential issues before listing it. Taking care of these
items in advance can help avoid unexpected surprises later
in the sales process.

October is the start of a new season of golf at
the Views Golf Club. The golf course has been
overseeded, so there are different colors of
green grass on the fairways and tees. This is a
good time to remind golfers about changes from
summer to winter play. The divot mix in the con-
tainers will be replaced with a sand and seed
mix that is only being used on fairways and tees that have
been overseeded. If that seed mix is placed in turf areas
that are not overseeded, it will eventually leave large green
patches in an otherwise brown turf. This applies to all of the
roughs and certain tee boxes that haven't been overseeded.
It's a small step to help reduce extra maintenance required
to chemically treat and remove the green spots throughout
the winter months. From a maintenance standpoint, it would
be better to do nothing with a divot in non-overseeded turf
than put seed mix in it.

When the golf course reopens following the overseed,
there will be a period of time with a cart path restriction. This
is due to the young ryegrass and wet conditions. Once the
cart path restriction is lifted, the areas on holes 2, 4, 9 and 14
that have been staked from cart path traffic the previous few
years will be restricted again. Since implementing these golf
cart traffic controls, the turf conditions in these specific areas
have dramatically improved resulting in minimal damage the
following summer.

Pyrography is the art of writing with fire. The dictionary de-
fines it as the art or technique of decorating wood or leather
by burning a design on the surface with a heated metalic
point. Our club has a dozen precision wood burners along
with dozens of different shape tips to acquire almost any de-
sign you may desire. It is important to be aware that burnt
wood objects will fade in due time if you do not protect the
design with an ultra violet finish. Look up the March issue
of the Tipster for info on UV products offering this type of
protection.
