Sun City Oro Valley would like to welcome you to the communi-
ty. You'll soon discover that living here is more than a lifestyle.

SCOV is a comfortable community of ideal size. There are 2,488
homes. More than 60% of our residents live here year round.
Most people cite the community's well-kept appearance, strong
financial position and the large number of activities available as
their reasons for moving here.

Sun City Oro Valley is located in the town of Oro Valley and sits
on a 1,000-acre site with 36% of the space allocated to common
area. A 6,715 yard par 72 golf course with nearby Catalina
mountains are the focal points. This handbook was created to
help you become more familiar with SCOV. We will cover a wide
range of topics within the pages of this handbook.

Should you have any questions or concerns, please ask our
friendly staff. The Administration Office is located at the Wel-
come Center and is open Monday-Friday, 7:30 AM — 4:30 PM.
Please stop by and say “Hi” or = 520-825-3711.

Facility Access Card/Key Fob

A facility access card is required to use the facilities, such as the
library, the Aquatic & Fitness Center and the other facilities that
are part of the community as well as participate in any clubs. This
card shows your name, address and unit/lot number. These
cards are non-transferable. A key fob fits on your keychain and
can also be used to gain entrance into the buildings and can be
purchased for $10.

Finances
We are a financially stable community. The community has been ag-
gressively planning for the future through the Asset Reserve pro-
gram. Its purpose is to provide funds for replacement and repair of
capital items. Go to scovaz.com>Members>Financials

Foundation

The Foundation has been created to provide voluntary financial sup-
port to meet qualified health, cultural, educational and charitable
needs of Southern Arizona, with priority attention to SCOV needs.
The creating of the Foundation Fund makes it possible for donors to
receive tax deductions for gifts contributed for these purposes. It is
the hope of the Foundation Council that there will be an increasing
level of giving, sharing and helping, which are powerful elements in
the evolving character of our community. See the Tipster for info.

RV Parking for Guests
Guests who bring an RV into our community may not park the RV
longer than six (6) consecutive hours within Sun City, and in no
case overnight (between the hours of 12 midnight and 6:00 a.m.),
except in a designated area when registered and a waiver signed
with the Association. Such parking is limited to 72 hours and in no

case shall such vehicle be occupied overnight. Call 520-917-8065
with any questions or more information.

Sun Shuttle Dial-A-Ride
Sun Shuttle is for Oro Valley residents 62 years of age and older
who are transportation dependent and/or eligible to ride under the
Americans for Disabilities Act. This is a pre-registered service.
Call 229-4990.
