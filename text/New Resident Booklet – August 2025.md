Welcome to Sun City Oro Valley!

We're delighted to have you join our community. You'll soon discover that living here is more than just a lifestyle—it’s an
experience.

SCOV is a warm, inviting community of ideal size, with 2,488 homes and more than 60% of our residents living here year-round.
People are drawn to our well-kept surroundings, strong financial foundation, and the wide variety of activities and amenities
available.

Nestled in the town of Oro Valley, our 1,000-acre community dedicates 36% of its space to beautiful common areas. At the heart of
SCOV is our scenic 6,715-yard, par-72 golf course, framed by stunning views of the nearby Catalina Mountains.

This handbook was created to help you get to know SCOV and all it has to offer. Inside, you'll find information on everything from
amenities to activities, along with tips for making the most of your new community.

If you have any questions or need assistance, our friendly staff are always happy to help. The Administration Office, located at the
Welcome Center, is open Monday-Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Please stop by to say hello—we look forward to meeting you!

BOARD MEMBERSHIP/RESPONSIBILITY

° Three-year term limit; public campaign, election in March; voting term begins in May
* No financial compensation
*¢ Committee liaison

The Board of Directors of Sun City Oro Valley Community Association, Inc. operates under the laws of the State of Arizona and in
accordance with the powers, duties and responsibilities provided in the governing documents of the Association. Accordingly, the
Board establishes corporate policy and assigns responsibilities for daily operations to the General Manager. The Board has the
ultimate responsibility for actions of the corporation and for maintaining, preserving and enhancing the assets and facilities of the
Association. The community members elected to serve in this important capacity must set aside their personal agendas, represent
the interests of the entire community, listen to input from all members, and take actions together that reflect their best
judgment for the good of the greater community. Each Board member has a personal fiduciary responsibility to avoid conflicts of
interest, avoid actions as a Board member outside of Board authority and protect the interests of the corporation.

For the list of directors, login to the members side of the website and select The Board or on page 2 of The Tipster.

FACILITY ACCESS CARD/KEY FOB

A facility access card is required to use the facilities, such as the library, the Aquatic & Fitness Center and the other facilities that are
part of the community as well as to participate in any clubs. This card shows your name, address and unit/lot number. These cards
are non-transferable. A key fob can be used to gain entrance into the buildings and can be purchased for $10.

FINANCES

We are a financially stable community. The community has been aggressively planning for the future through the Asset Reserve
program. Its purpose is to provide funds for replacement and repair of capital items. Go to scovaz.com, login and from the Members
home page select Financials.

FOUNDATION

The Foundation has been created to provide voluntary financial support to meet qualified health, cultural, educational and
charitable needs of Southern Arizona, with priority attention to SCOV needs. The creation of the Foundation Fund makes it possible
for donors to receive tax deductions for gifts contributed for these purposes. It is the hope of the Foundation Council that there will
be an increasing level of giving, sharing and helping, which are powerful elements in the evolving character of our community. See
the Tipster for info.

WANT TO RENT YOUR HOME?

1. Complete application to become a rental. We are limited to 249 units in the Association. Submit to the Administration Office

the forms found on our website under Members Services>For Your Home>Rent Your Home.

A lease may not be less than 30 days.

3. Your Member’s Facility Access Cards are non-transferable and may only be used by you. You may not give your cards to
renters.

4. The Non-Owner Occupied Home form and Age Verification must be completed by the homeowner and returned to our
office at least 3 business days prior to your renter’s arrival. Failure to submit the paperwork will result in fines and
denying access cards to your renter.

5. There is a $25 renter registration fee which covers two Facility Access cards for the length of the lease. Additional cards
will be charged $25/card per month. Each person must have their own card.

6. Administration does not keep rental agreements on file.

RV PARKING FOR GUESTS & FOR YOU

Guests bringing an RV into Sun City may park it for no more than six (6) consecutive hours within the community, and never
overnight (midnight to 6 am), except in a designated area approved by the Association. To use a designated area, the guest must
register and sign a waiver with the Association. Parking in a designated area is limited to 72 hours, and the RV may not be occupied
overnight at any time. For residents, see the Owner & Resident Handbook for RV guidelines. For questions or more information, call

520-917-8065.

SUN SHUTTLE DIAL-A-RIDE
Sun Shuttle is for Oro Valley residents 62 years of age and older who are transportation dependent and/or eligible to ride under the
Americans for Disabilities Act. This is a pre-registered service. Call 520-229-4990.
