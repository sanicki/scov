From The Pro Shop

We Win Again!

Once again, thanks to your support and an
outstanding job by all the staff, The Views
Golf Club was voted The Best Golf Course
in the Tucson area. We edged out some
stiff competition in Ventana Canyon and
SaddleBrook 1 to win the 2019 Arizona Daily
Star Reader's Choice Award. Thanks for
voting for us and more importantly for
playing your golf here at The Views.

Overseed is around the Corner

Enjoy the excellent conditions and our
stunning views while you can. September is
the time of the year we plant the winter
ryegrass on the golf course for the upcoming
season. It requires the assistance of all
residents to make sure not to drive golf carts
or walk on the golf course while the new
grass seed is being planted and watered.
The golf course will be closed during over-
seeding starting Monday, September 23 and
will open back up for play on Monday,
October 14.

The September Pro Shop hours will be open
for regular hours 6 AM - 5 PM through
Sunday, September 23. We will be closed
September 24 & 25 and re-open for limited
hours 9 AM -12:00 PM September 26 -
October 13. During over-seeding, the range
will be open, and practice balls will be hit off
mats only beginning September 26.

Now is an excellent time to purchase a
range card. You can buy 20 large buckets
for $150 and save $2.50 per bucket or 50
large buckets for $300 a savings of $4 per
bucket off the regular price!

Once we open for play on October 14, all
golf carts must remain on the cart path only
for a couple of weeks. The projected date for
golf carts to start driving on the grass will be
Tuesday, October 29.

Coming Saturday, October 12 will be our
2nd Annual Run the Views 5K. You can
choose to walk or run the course and
experience the golf course from a whole
different perspective. The event will take
place on holes 1-10. All interested
participants may sign up on our website
www.theviewsgolfclub.com.

The Etiquette Corner

Quiet on the Tee! Ever play with that
someone who although the fairway or green
is clear they keep on talking about whatever
...... Proper etiquette on the golf course is to
play away when it is safe. Wrap up your
story or say “to be continued” when it’s clear
to hit away. Or better yet, save it for the
19th hole.

Pet Peeves

The pet peeve for September is the
dwindling supply of range baskets. Range
baskets are not to be taken off the range.
Range balls are provided for the chipping
area. Range balls should not be taken from
the range for future use. Thank you for
eliminating the pet peeve of the month!

Tip from the Pro

The best putting tip | ever received was from
my best friend’s dad. | was struggling with
the putter and becoming frustrated...sound
familiar? He told me, “You know Tom, when
you putt there are only two outcomes. You'll
either make it or you won't.” Sounds a bit
trite, but | began to stop beating myself up,
relax more and developed more patience
with my putts not all falling. So, remember
only one of two things will happen when you
putt. I’m hoping for the good thing! Good
luck!
