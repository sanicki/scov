New in November

November is here and so are a lot of
familiar faces. Welcome back to all our
seasonal residents. We also have a new
face in the pro shop. Joseph Courtney has
joined our staff as an Assistant Professional.
Joseph comes to us from Elkhorn Ridge
Golf Club in Spearfish, South Dakota. Good
luck Joseph! We also welcome back
Charley Young as a Pro Shop Attendant
AND as our Master Griller. Look for the
“Grill on the Range” on Fridays and
Saturdays 11 AM -3 PM.

Course Maintenance

We would like to pass along a job well done
to our Course Superintendent, Mike Kropf,
and his crew for a successful overseed.
The course is in great shape!. Let’s all help
keep it that way by filling our divots and
fixing our ball marks and at least one other
on the greens.

October Results

October was a busy month with many
events. Here are results of the major events
in October.

The Views 5K Run/Walk

A total of 86 runners and_ walkers
participated in The Views 5K which was
held on the front nine and 10" hole of our
golf course. In the Women’s Division, Celia
Schreker crossed the line first with a time of
28:58. Men’s Division winner was Michael
Prow with a time of 26:01. Everyone
commented on how beautiful our course

was and look forward to this being an
annual event. Plans are underway for a
bigger and better Run the Views 5K in 2019.

Glow Ball Tournament

A field of over 30 players descended on our
putting green to participate in a 3-hole
putting and 1-hole chipping contest. The
course was “lit up” with glow paint outlining
the holes and flags by our resident
“pyrotechnician’”, Tim Lochner. A great time
was had by all. Leaderboard & results:

Cart & Treat Results

About 16 golf carts spookily appeared in the
cart parking lot on October 28. The golf
carts were decorated in Halloween fare and
the owners brought along candy to pass out
to the many children (and some adults) that
attended. After a long and arduous judging,
the winners of the best cart decoration were
Gary and Nancy Gibson and their hearse
golf cart. Best costumes went to Bozo the
Clown (aka, O. David Johnson) and
Mistress of the Day of the Dead (aka. Susan
Olson). A special mention must be given to
best dog costume to Rosie the dog. Proud
owners Art & Roberta Bruchez.
