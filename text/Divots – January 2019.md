Golf 45 is Here!

After a trial run of Golf 45 with our Casual
Golf Group and the mostly positive
responses we received, we have decided to
offer Golf 45 on Saturday 1/19/2019 as a
standalone group. This is a new endeavor
and is perfect for the beginning golfer, the
chip and putter who wants more, or the
advanced golfer wanting to sharpen their
approach shots and short game. We will
shotgun start at 3pm. The object of Golf 45 is
to play 9 holes in under 2 hours teeing off
from the 100-yard marker (or on Par 3’s from
the forward tee) AND shoot 45 or under. If
you accomplish that, the next time you play
Golf 45 you move to the 150-yard marker.
Accomplishing that moves you to the 200-
yard marker and when you shoot 45 and paly
under 2 hours you graduate to play the
course from the normal tee boxes. This is a
great way to gradually manage the game of
golf and improve your skills at the same time.
The fee for playing Golf 45 will be $15 and
the field will be limited to 56 players. For
more info and to sign up please call the pro
shop.
