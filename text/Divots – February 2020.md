FEBRUARY 2020
From the Pro Shop

We are off to a great 2020! It was a banner
year in 2019 for The Views and we want to
thank you all our members, residents and
guest for your support. We look forward to
serving you all through 2020 and provide
the professional friendly service, excellent
conditions and a memorable golfing
experience you have come to expect.

After a long run we are changing the
monthly newsletter. The new name moving
forward will be called Tee to Green. We
are proud of the day to day conditions and
want the newsletter name to reflect that.
We hope you will enjoy the new newsletter
and look forward to communicating to you
all the happenings here at The Views Golf
Club. Speaking of new, the Pro Shop now
carries Medterra Brand CBD. Many PGA
golfers such as Bubba Watson and Charley
Hoffman use CBD to help them focus and
have energy all through their rounds. For
more info check with the staff in the pro
shop.

Great shots @ The Views

Congratulations to Ted Sorich on not only
shooting his age but beating his age by 5
shots! Ted shot an eighteen-hole total of 6
over par 78! You can do the math to find
his age! You inspire us, Ted!

Our twelfth hole is arguably our hardest
par 3. Tim Kelley may no longer think so.
Tim aced number 12 from 166 yards with a
3 wood. Rumor has it the shot was hit
directly into a 40-mph wind. Either way it
was a great shot and one that Tim will
always remember. Way to go Tim!

Talking about making an ACE. Robert
Taylor holed out a Pitching Wedge on hole
#3 from 125 on January 31.

Results

On January 11-12 the Views Golf Club
hosted the Oro Valley Senior
Championship. In the Championship
Division Defending champion Jon
Lindstrom from Broomfield, CO repeated
as champion with a 36-hole score of 5
under par 139. Richard Bradsby finished
2°4 at 145, Tim Freeh was 3 at 152.

In the White/Gold Division Jay Anderson
from Helena, MT captured 1* place with a
7 over par 151 for 36 holes followed
closely by Tim Blau, Lance DeNardin and
Robin McCool at 152. In the Gold Division
Trent Olney captured the Gross Division
with a 36-hole total of 157 while the Net
Division went to The Views own Chris
Puffer at 157. As one can see this
tournament draws players from all over the
USA.

January 18th, we held our annual Welcome
Back Demo Day & Helicopter Ball Drop
extravaganza. Charley Young & Katelyn
Hutchison dropped over 600 golf balls
from a helicopter hovering hundreds of
feet in the air over a target on the driving
range. The three closest golf balls were in
third place Pam Shanahan $300 dollars,
Mike Friend second place $700 and
Margot Chatterton in first place (14inches)
$1,200. This popular event helped raise
money for all the Women’s and Men’s
clubs’ associations. Thank you all for your
support of this event and Welcome Back!
