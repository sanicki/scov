SHORT GAME AND PUTTING

| know we are all looking forward to playing on our newly constructed greens and
surrounds. After playing on our temporary greens for the last 5 months we've probably
changed our putting stroke to account for the slower greens. With the shorter course
we've also become used to hitting short irons into the greens.

Here are two tips and some encouragement to help you on the new course. First keep

## Page 8

practicing those short irons. You have been hitting them a lot lately because of the shorter
holes, you may need them to save par on the longer par 4’s now. Your short game (from
100 yards and in) will become much more important now. Use the same swing but used
different clubs from 100 yards and in. Notice how differently the ball reacts based on the
club used.

Our new short game area should help you sharpen up the chipping and pitching. As with
chipping and pitching, your putting game will face new challenges with the new greens.
Focus on distance for both the chipping and putting. Distance control is #1 when putting. If
you get the distance right you'll almost never three putt. To help with distance control put a
ball in your hand and pick a target hole on the green that is 30+ feet away. Toss the ball
underhand, like bowling, and see how close you can get. Do this a few times until you get
that ball in a 3-foot circle around the hole. Now take your putter and use that same
tempo/force you did with your golf bowling ball. Put a smooth full stroke on it and watch it
get close or who knows, maybe go in!

Good Read

Scientists who study the human brain say that the stream of consciousness can absorb
and apply only one thought at a time. This is certainly applicable in the Golf swing. Most of
us would agree it is easy to have multiple thoughts or ideas attempting to work themselves
out in a single swing. If all of this is true then as a Golfer it would seem focusing on one
area of improvement would be the only efficient way to improve. For those seeking to
improve this season focus on one thing to improve upon in each practice session. We
promise if you bring this intention to every swing you will look back at this season as your
best ever!
