GOLF CART POLICY

The Views Golf Club golf cart policy is two players per golf cart. Single players should be
prepared to share a Views Club cart. We pair golfers up to share a golf cart according to
starting times booked. Cart rental is a per seat charge.

Due to the lack of availability of golf carts, we cannot provide single rider golf carts. You
may consider walking or renting one of our four single-rider scooters. However, please do
not book a tee time if you are uncomfortable riding with someone else in a cart. Masks are
recommended while riding in the golf cart. Masks are not required while playing, on the
driving range or practice areas, if maintaining social distance — but if unable to do so,
please wear your mask.

If you have a private golf cart, you may continue to ride as a single. Only two carts will be

Lesson Tee

Forget about how you used to play

Stay present and take what the game gives you now. If that is 220 yards off the tee, get
your fairway woods and hybrids out and do the best you can with the longer approach
shots. Always over club!

Work on your scoring game

If you have recently lost flexibility and strength, it does not have to affect your game from
100 yards and closer. You can sharpen your chipping and putting skills to make up for
any lost distance.

Mind & Body

The mind/body connection is vital for golfers. And... the results speak for themselves!
Staying as flexible and as healthy as you can for as long as possible is essential to
playing better golf. Also, walk and carry whenever possible!

Get properly fitted

Not only do we play golf dreaming of yesteryear, but we also often let our pride affect our
game. Many golfers say, or more accurately, using equipment that once fit their game. 85-
90 mph clubhead speed does not likely require a stiff shaft, 9 degrees of loft, or 75 grams
of weight to achieve proper launch and landing conditions. Proper fitting golf equipment
demands being brutally honest with yourself and listening to the recommendation of the
professional club fitter

Consider swing “adjustments,” not a new swings

As a teacher of many years, | know this much: The swing you have had for so many years
will not change very much. This does not mean it cannot be made more effective. Your
swing may need to be “tweaked,” not rebuilt.

Play tees further foward

To make the game more enjoyable, consider playing tee further forward to allow you to hit
greens in regulation. As a PGA golf professional, | was, at one time, a fairly decent player.
Now over 60 years young, | enjoy the game more, not playing from the back tees. And in
a few years, | will likely move up again. It is just a fact of life, and denying it is futile.

Check your fundamentals

Just because a specific grip, posture, or ball position was effective once, as we age, all
these may need adjustments from time to time. Swings get shorter, slower, narrow, etc.
And as they do, we must allow for these things and find new ways to complement our
