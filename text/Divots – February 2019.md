Winter Weather

Does it seem like we have had an unusually
cold and wet winter this year? While we have
had only a few frost delays, it’s important to
review details regarding the cold weather.
The frost line is 520-825-0322. This line is
updated no later than 6:30 am. Tee times
that have been affected by frost delays may
be adjusted as we catch up times caused by
cancellations. Please check our website
(theviewsgolfclub.com) for up-dated tee
times. To keep you warm throughout the
winter months, check out the new Views
women’s and men’s winter knit hats. We also
just received men’s and women’s fleece
jackets and pull overs. Pass holders will
receive 25% off these jackets and pullovers.
Stay warm and make a fashion statement all
at the same time.

Course Maintenance

With the increase in rounds here at The
Views comes the increase in ball marks and
divots. Let’s all do our part by obeying the
gate system and by fixing our ball marks (and
one other) and filling our divots. If we all do
this, we will continue to have the excellent
conditions we have become accustomed to
at The Views.

From the Pro Shop
The Oro Valley Senior Championship

The Views Golf Club at Oro Valley hosted the
2019 Oro Valley Senior Championships
January 12-13. A field of 48 players
participated in 3 different flights.

The Championship Flight (gross only) winner
was Jon Lindstrom from Lakewood, CO. Jon
shot 5 under par 139 for a convincing 8-shot
victory over The Views’ own Tom Beach.

In the White/Gold Division Robin McCool
from Center Valley, PA, bested a field of 23
to take low gross honors with a 36-hole total
of 2 under 142. Hans Birkholz from
Scottsdale, AZ, won low net honors with a
score of 10 under 134. The Views Bill
Cocanour finished 1 stroke back to claim 2°
place honors.

In the Gold Division, Steve Stuebner from the
Gallery Golf Club won the Gross Division
with a score of 19 over par 163. The net
Division finished with a tie at 1 under par 143
between Andy Callahan from Lakewood,
CO, and the Views’ own John Crittenden.
Thank you to all who participated, and we
hope to see you back again in 2020.

Play with the Pros Finals

On January 15, the Play with the Pro’s Winter
Finals took place. Teams advancing to the
finals were Rick’s team with Preston Sheriff,
Denny Garren and Kevin Devaney; Tim’s
team with Bill Cocanour, Kurt Klucking and
John Rowe; Joe’s team with Art Scarla, Bob
Howe, and Vince Cardinal; and Tom’s team
w/ Doug Kimble, Sherry Linker and Richard
Flax. When the dust settled, Tom’s team won
the Winter Series with a score of 14 under
par 130, besting Joe’s team in a scorecard
playoff.

Golf Fair & Helicopter Drop

The most exciting event in January took
place on the January 19 at our Annual Golf
Fair & Helicopter Ball Drop. The driving
range was overflowing with guests eager to
see which of the 630 golf balls purchased
would be the lucky winners.

The 3 ball-drop winners were in 3rd place
George Lindsay $400; 2nd place Warren
Witler $750: and first place, whose ball was
holed out from the helicopter for a hole in one
was Helen Bruning taking home the grand
prize of $1200. We had a beautiful day for
this event, and everyone was a winner as all
Women's & Men’s associations raised over
$2000 combined for their respective
associations. The Women’s _ 18-hole
association was the top group with a total of
$585 raised! Wait until next year!

A Word from the Pro

The word for this month is FITNESS.
Adhering to a consistent fitness program will
reap benefits for you on the golf course.
Injury prevention and greater stamina are
two of those benefits. It's also important to
train our minds with positive self-talk instead
of berating ourselves for poor performance.
Apositive outlook and positive reinforcement
will go a long way towards more consistent
play and more FUN on the course. Get fit and
stay fit!
