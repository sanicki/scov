PACE OF PLAY

| have received several questions lately
about what is expected of players as far as
“pace of play” on our golf course. First of all,
our new 9 hole time frame is 2 hours and 5
minutes. It is very important that you keep
up with the group in front of you.

Also, remember that there will be twosomes,
threesomes and foursomes on the course
and also golfers only playing 9 holes. Gaps
will occur because of this; however they
should tighten up as long as everyone is
playing within the allotted time.

Please listen to the Rangers when they ask
you to pick up the pace, pick up your ball or
skip a hole as they are only doing their job.
Hitting in to the group in front of you and or
yelling at them will_not be tolerated.
